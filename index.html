FAQ

Questions We Get Asked a Lot

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. After a 30-minute call we send you a fixed-price proposal with a clear scope, timeline and milestones, so there are no surprises.

How long does it take?

Landing pages and marketing sites usually take 2-4 weeks. A web or mobile MVP typically takes 8-12 weeks. Larger platforms are planned in phases so you can launch early and keep building.

Who owns the code?

You do. 100% of the code, designs and accounts (repositories, hosting, App Store) belong to you from day one. If you need one, we can also sign an NDA.

How do we communicate, and what about time zones?

We have a presence in New York and Mexico City and work with clients anywhere in the world. We set meeting times that work in your time zone, and if you're in the US or Mexico, we work your hours. You get a dedicated project manager, regular progress demos and a direct channel to the team, in English or Spanish.

What happens after launch?

We stay with you. Ongoing support (monitoring, fixes, updates and new features) is available through a monthly maintenance plan, sized to what your product needs.

Can you add AI to my existing product?

Yes. We integrate AI into existing products and internal tools (assistants, smart search, data extraction and automation), starting with the use case that pays off fastest.
